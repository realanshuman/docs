The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets AI assistants, such as Claude or Cursor, connect to outside tools. A Suresign MCP server will let those assistants work with your workspace in the same careful way the built-in [agent](/agent/overview) does.

<Info>
  MCP support is coming soon. This page will be updated with setup steps when it is available.
</Info>

## What it is for

If you already use an AI assistant at work, MCP lets you ask it about your agreements without switching apps. For example: "Which documents are still waiting for a signature?"

## What will not change

The same rules that keep the built-in agent safe are the product's rules, not a prompt's:

- An assistant acts as you, with your role and permissions, never more.
- Anything that reaches another person waits for your approval.
- Every step is recorded in the document's [audit trail](/proof/audit-trail-and-certificate).
- Your documents are never used to train any model.
